#Computer-Vision  #Image-Recognition 

> To evaluate how good a classifier is.
$$
\text{Sensitivity (TP Rate)}=\frac{TP}{TP+FN}
$$
$$
\text{Specificity (FP Rate)}=\frac{FP}{FP+TN}
$$

ROC is plot of TPR (y-axis) vs FPR (x-axis) *where each point corresponds to a threshold value*. The area under the curve (AUC) is the probability of TP and is used to measure how good a classifier is.
