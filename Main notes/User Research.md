# Must consider
Our test [[The H (Human) in HCI|users]] can be:
- The actual final users who would use the system personally or professionally who probably used a previous version of the product; or
- The potential final users which have some of the same characteristics as the real ones.
Our usage context:
- Public: Immediate use.
- Entertainment: Attractive.
- Industry: Productive.
- Critical: Zero risk.
Our task's characteristics: Repetitive, regular, occasional, sensitive to environmental changes, time-constrained, risky...
Our technical constraints: Platform, memory, devices.
