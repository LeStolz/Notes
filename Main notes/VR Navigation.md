#Extended-Reality 

> Consider ease of learning and use, comfort, speed, accuracy, spatial awareness, the user’s understanding of their position and orientation in the VE, the ability to gather information during navigation, and the sense of presence.

Traveling: Mobile, change of perspective.

Wayfinding: Cognitive, where am I? where do I want to go? how to?

Exploring: Navigating without explicit goal.

Goal finding: Naive (unknown position), Targeted (known position), Assisted (position known by the system). Build knowledge of object positions by moving toward them.

Manoeuvre: Quick and precise movement, slight change in POV for a specific task. Due to tracking limitations and cable clutter, it is preferable to perform this task explicitly.

Problems:
- Simple viewpoint movement.
- Speed/acceleration control using gestures, voice commands, etc.
- Rotation needed for mono- or multi-plane projections (CAVE, single screen), not HMD.
- Constraint movement to follow terrain.
- Decide when and how motion starts/stops—e.g., click to start/stop, press to start/release to stop, or auto-stop at a predefined location.
