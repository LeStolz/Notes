#HCI 

> Sight is like a [[Camera Models|Pinhole camera]]. 70% of sensory receptors are vision.

FOV:
- eye fixed: 90° sideways (temple), 45° inward (nose); 45° up, 70° down.
- head moving: 200° sideways, 130° inward; 140° up, 170° down
Immersion FOV of 180 deg horizontal, 150 vertical, HD FOV of 15 deg.
Peripheral vision (movement oriented) of 180 deg.
Distance intra-pupilar 53-73mm
Resolutions: 0.14 deg.
Visual acuity:
- Illuminated slit on black: 30 arcseconds
- Two bright points on black: 1 arcminute
- Two dark points on light: 2 arcminutes

120M Rods to perceive B/W. 1000x more sensitive than cones
6-7M Cones: 64% R, 32% G, 2% B.
1700 colors and 300 shades of gray => 2–8 million colors.
Blind spot is where there is no sensory cells.

Minimum frequency: 14 - 20 Hz.
Retinal persistence: 1/30 to 1/50 second

Stereopsis:
- Monocular (color, brightness, shape, relative dimension, movement, perspective, interposition)
- Binocular (size, parallax, depth):
	- Ocular (eyes orientation) (convergence)
	- Cilar (crystaline modification) (accomodation)
	- Retinal disparity caused by the non-convergence of the visual optical axes, Zero disparity when the optical axes converge
	- > The superimposition of the two different images provides information about depth.
