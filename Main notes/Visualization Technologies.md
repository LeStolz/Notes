## Display Technologies
Screens: CRT (old) > LCD (backlight filtered by liquid crystals) > Plasma > OLED (pixels emit their own light individually).

Projections: 
- CRT (good resolution, contrast, weak luminosity) => dark rooms, stereo active
- (Reflective) LCD (low cost, persistent) => stereo passive 
- DLP good everything, high cost => stereo passive or active, light rooms, numeric cinema.
- 1 chip ((Reflective) LCD) = one sensor for all colors; 3 chips (CRT) = separate sensors for each color → better image quality.
- LCD 50% aperture, Reflective LCD 93% aperture, DLP 88% aperture. More is narrower pixel gap => better.

Projection light sources:
- Halogen: LCD chip, 1000 hours.
- Xenon: DLP or Reflective LCD, 1000 hours, 20 000 lumens.
- Laser: DLP or Reflective LCD, 12000 hours, lumen > Xenon.
- LED: UV to IR (240–900 nm) with uses like sterilization, displays, traffic lights, and remote/night-vision devices.
# HMD
90% [[Virtual Reality]] still use spatial screens as HMD needs to consider:
- Resolution, FOV, focus distance.
- Tracking, lighting.
- Conform, encumberment.
See-through [[Augmented Reality]] mostly use HMD or mobile, not spatial (projection onto real objects).
