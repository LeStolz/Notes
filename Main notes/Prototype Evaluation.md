#HCI 

> To fix or discard ASAP.

To check if your design meets requirements:
- Time, Money, Happiness
- Productive, error-free, safe
- Good public image
- Repeat customers
Measured [[Quantitative Evaluation|quantitatively]], [[Qualitative Evaluation|qualitatively]], objectively, or subjectively in lab or in real environment.
Expected Outcome: Work? Why? Why not?
A multi-disciplinary approach.
# Look for these in evaluation
- Interface flaws: Confusing, misleading, inefficient interaction flow.
- Missed opportunities: functionalities users can’t find or don’t use as intended.
- Conformity to standards.
- Feedbacks to improve the product.
- Ergonomic audit.
# Two general evaluation approaches
Formative testing:
Done at early stages of development to explore (qualitative) general design choices.
Summative testing:
Done at later stages to test specific (quantitative and goal-oriented) interface items.
*No clear dividing line between them*
