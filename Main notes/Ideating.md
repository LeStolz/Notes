# Brainstorming
## Creativity and Dissent
Authentic dissenters – people who **really** (as in real and not deliberate) disagree with group – can enhance group creativity. Their opinion needn’t be right but can free the group from stagnant thinking. The originality of the minority stimulates the majority

*Embrace differences: Personal desires drive changes and innovation*

*HCI is an effort to surpass being human. But we work in group, you can't do everything by yourself*:
- POV: Know yours, understand others.
- Communicate: Explain; persuade; synthesize.
- Experiment: Learn; document; progress.
## Brainstorming is for everyone
Including your end users in focus groups to help discuss design choices => Facilitate user's acceptance.
## Critical thinking vs. Lateral thinking
- Critical: Judges true/false value of a statement, identifying errors.
- Lateral: Judges “movement value” of a statement, moving from one known idea to creating new ideas.
## You must
- *Ask How might we...\<Something specific\>...?*
- Improvise: Listen, say the first thing that comes to your mind reflexively.
